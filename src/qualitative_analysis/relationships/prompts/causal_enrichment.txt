You are an expert in Causal Analysis and Logic.

Your task is to analyze a specific relationship between two entities and determine if it represents a **Causal Relationship** (Cause-Effect).
If it is causal, you must determine the **Polarity**, **Certainty**, and whether the causality is **Explicit** or **Implicit**.

### INPUT DATA
**Source Entity**: {source}
**Target Entity**: {target}
**Relationship Type**: {relationship}
**Evidence Snippets**:
{evidence}

### DEFINITIONS

1.  **Causal Relationship**: The Source entity actively influences, produces, triggers, or causes a change in the state of the Target entity.
    *   *Examples*: "Rain causes flooding", "Interest rates reduce inflation", "Virus triggers immune response".
    *   **NOT Causal**:
        *   **Prerequisites/Requirements**: "A requires B", "A needs B". (This is dependency, not causality).
        *   **Attributes**: "Apple is a company".
        *   **Locations**: "Paris is in France".
        *   **Correlations**: "A is related to B" (without direction of influence).

2.  **Polarity**:
    *   **Positive**: The Source *increases*, *promotes*, *stimulates*, *enables*, or *causes* the Target to happen/increase.
    *   **Negative**: The Source *decreases*, *inhibits*, *prevents*, *blocks*, or *reduces* the Target.
    *   **Neutral**: The Source causes the Target, but the direction is unspecified or complex.

3.  **Certainty**:
    *   **Certain**: The text states it as a fact (e.g., "causes", "leads to", "results in").
    *   **Likely**: The text suggests it is probable (e.g., "suggests", "likely", "estimated to").
    *   **Possible**: The text is speculative or conditional (e.g., "may", "could", "might").

4.  **Explicit vs Implicit**:
    *   **Explicit**: The text uses direct causal verbs (e.g., "causes", "leads to", "results in", "triggers", "prevents").
    *   **Implicit**: The causality is inferred from context or sequence (e.g., "The storm passed. The streets were flooded." -> Implicitly causal).

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Return a single JSON object. The `reasoning` field MUST come first and contain your step-by-step analysis BEFORE you determine your answers.

*   `reasoning` (string): Your step-by-step analysis of the evidence. Consider: What verbs are used? Is there a cause-effect relationship or just a dependency/requirement? What is the direction of influence?
*   `is_causal` (boolean): True if the relationship is causal (and NOT just a prerequisite).
*   `polarity` (string): "positive", "negative", or "neutral" (only if is_causal is True, else null).
*   `certainty` (string): "certain", "likely", or "possible" (only if is_causal is True, else null).
*   `explicit_vs_implicit` (string): "explicit" or "implicit" (only if is_causal is True, else null).
