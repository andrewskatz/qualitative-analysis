CRITICAL - POLARITY CLASSIFICATION:

Polarity describes the SEMANTIC RELATIONSHIP between the factor and the decision, NOT whether the factor is mentioned as a reason in the text. Just because the text says "because X" does NOT mean X is supporting. You must reason about what the factor MEANS for the decision:

- supporting: The factor is a BENEFIT, MOTIVATION, or POSITIVE REASON that favors or justifies the decision.
- opposing: The factor is a RISK, COST, CONCERN, CONSTRAINT, or NEGATIVE CONSEQUENCE that argues against the decision or represents a downside.
- neutral: The factor is tangential, mixed, or has no clear directional relationship.

CRITICAL: Do NOT assume "because X" means X is supporting. The text structure "decided to do X because Y" sometimes means "Y is a challenge/concern that factored into the decision" rather than "Y is a benefit." You must evaluate the semantic content of the factor independently.

Ask yourself: "Does this factor make the decision MORE attractive (supporting) or does it represent a COST, RISK, or DOWNSIDE of the decision (opposing)?"
